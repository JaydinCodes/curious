PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY

Privacy

The catalogue keeps personal learning data in your browser and records only anonymous aggregate product events.

01

What stays in your browser

Completed topics, started topics, notes, pins and streaks are stored locally. Notes are never sent by analytics. Manual export files contain your learning data because you asked the browser to create them.

02

What aggregate analytics records

Counts of searches, random draws, completed topic codes and resource clicks. Search text is never transmitted; only broad query-length and result-count buckets are sent. Resource URLs, notes, sync codes, names, email addresses and user identifiers are not collected.

03

No behavioural profile

The analytics endpoint does not set cookies and does not store IP addresses, user agents, advertising identifiers or cross-site identifiers. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable analytics automatically.

04

Your analytics choice

You can disable or re-enable aggregate analytics for this browser below. Core catalogue features continue to work either way.
